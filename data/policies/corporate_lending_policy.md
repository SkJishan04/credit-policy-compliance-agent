# Corporate Lending Policy

Policy Version: 3.1
Effective Date: 2025-06-01

## Loan-to-Value Limits for Secured Corporate Facilities

```yaml
section_id: CORP-6.1
title: Loan-to-Value Limits for Secured Corporate Facilities
borrower_type: Corporate
max_ltv_ratio: 0.70
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: null
min_credit_score: null
required_documents:
  - Certificate of incorporation
  - Board resolution authorizing borrowing
  - Audited financials (last 3 years)
  - Collateral valuation report
```

Secured corporate lending facilities are capped at a 70% Loan-to-Value ratio, reflecting the higher
risk-weighting assigned to corporate exposures under the internal capital adequacy framework. Any
facility above this threshold requires escalation to the Corporate Credit Committee.

## Unsecured Corporate Credit Line Cap

```yaml
section_id: CORP-6.2
title: Unsecured Corporate Credit Line Cap
borrower_type: Corporate
max_ltv_ratio: null
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: 50
min_credit_score: null
required_documents:
  - Certificate of incorporation
  - Audited financials (last 3 years)
  - Credit rating report
```

Unsecured corporate credit lines may not exceed ₹50 lakhs without a minimum external credit rating of
BBB or equivalent. Facilities above this cap must be structured as secured term loans.

## Governance and Board Approval Requirements

```yaml
section_id: CORP-6.3
title: Governance and Board Approval Requirements
borrower_type: Corporate
max_ltv_ratio: null
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: null
min_credit_score: null
required_documents:
  - Board resolution authorizing borrowing
  - Certificate of incorporation
```

Every corporate borrowing application must be accompanied by a board resolution explicitly authorizing
the transaction. Applications lacking this document cannot proceed to final compliance sign-off.
