# Retail Individual Lending Policy

Policy Version: 1.8
Effective Date: 2025-01-15

## Loan-to-Value Limits for Secured Retail Loans

```yaml
section_id: RETAIL-2.1
title: Loan-to-Value Limits for Secured Retail Loans
borrower_type: Retail Individual
max_ltv_ratio: 0.80
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: null
min_credit_score: 700
required_documents:
  - PAN card
  - Salary slips (last 3 months)
  - Collateral valuation report
```

Secured retail individual loans, such as those backed by property or fixed deposits, may not exceed
an 80% Loan-to-Value ratio. Applicants must have a minimum CIBIL-equivalent credit score of 700 for
this facility to be considered compliant.

## Unsecured Personal Loan Cap

```yaml
section_id: RETAIL-2.2
title: Unsecured Personal Loan Cap
borrower_type: Retail Individual
max_ltv_ratio: null
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: 5
min_credit_score: 720
required_documents:
  - PAN card
  - Salary slips (last 3 months)
  - Bank statements (last 6 months)
```

Unsecured personal loans to retail individuals are capped at ₹5 lakhs. Applicants with a credit score
below 720 are not eligible for unsecured facilities under this policy and must offer collateral.

## Income Verification Requirements

```yaml
section_id: RETAIL-2.3
title: Income Verification Requirements
borrower_type: Retail Individual
max_ltv_ratio: null
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: null
min_credit_score: null
required_documents:
  - Salary slips (last 3 months)
  - Form 16
```

All retail individual applicants must provide income verification through salary slips and Form 16.
Self-employed applicants must additionally submit the last 2 years of ITR in place of salary slips.
