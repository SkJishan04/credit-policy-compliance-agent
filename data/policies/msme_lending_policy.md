# MSME Lending Policy

Policy Version: 2.3
Effective Date: 2025-04-01

## Loan-to-Value Limits for Secured MSME Loans

```yaml
section_id: MSME-4.1
title: Loan-to-Value Limits for Secured MSME Loans
borrower_type: MSME
max_ltv_ratio: 0.75
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: null
min_credit_score: 650
required_documents:
  - GST registration certificate
  - Last 2 years ITR
  - Collateral valuation report
```

For secured MSME loans, the maximum permissible Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio is 75% of the assessed
collateral value. Loan officers must independently verify the collateral valuation report before
approving any facility above this threshold. Exceptions require sanction from the Regional Credit
Committee and must be documented in the loan file.

## Unsecured MSME Lending Cap

```yaml
section_id: MSME-4.2
title: Unsecured MSME Lending Cap
borrower_type: MSME
max_ltv_ratio: null
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: 10
min_credit_score: 700
required_documents:
  - GST registration certificate
  - Last 2 years ITR
  - Business continuity proof (min. 3 years)
```

Unsecured MSME loans, i.e. facilities extended without any collateral, are capped at ₹10 lakhs per
borrower under this policy. Any request exceeding this cap must be restructured as a secured facility
or escalated for Corporate Credit Committee review. Minimum credit score for unsecured MSME lending
is 700.

## Documentation Standards for MSME Applicants

```yaml
section_id: MSME-4.3
title: Documentation Standards for MSME Applicants
borrower_type: MSME
max_ltv_ratio: null
max_unsecured_loan_lakhs: null
min_credit_score: null
required_documents:
  - GST registration certificate
  - Udyam registration certificate
  - Last 2 years audited financials
```

All MSME applicants must submit a valid Udyam registration certificate in addition to standard KYC
documentation. Loan files missing this document cannot be marked compliant regardless of LTV or
loan amount.
